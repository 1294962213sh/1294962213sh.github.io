Questions

Do my guests need an app or an account?

No. They tap the link, type their name, and reply. It works on any phone.

What if the date isn't settled?

Give me two or three options. Guests pick every date that works and see the running tally. Once you choose, tell me and the page switches to a normal RSVP with calendar buttons. Same link.

Can I see who replied?

Yes. You get a private guest list that updates by itself: one line per person, their latest answer, and the headcount. Guests only ever see counts.

Can I change things after it's sent?

Reply to the email with the change. The link stays the same, so everyone sees the update when they next open it.

How fast, really?

Your preview is in your inbox within 24 hours of your request, usually sooner. Say yes, pay $15, and the live link follows within a few hours — in time for a party this weekend if you ask by Thursday.

Can I ask for a particular style?

Yes — that's the point. Tell me an artist (Monet, Matisse, Hockney), a fashion or jewelry house (Chanel, Dior, Cartier), an art style (impressionism, art deco) or just a feeling (a Paris café at dusk), and the invitation is drawn in that spirit: its palette, its kind of line, its mood. Never a logo, never a copy; always yours.
