Each day, 5 minutes

  1. Post that day's image with its caption (all images are 4:5 — choose the 4:5 crop if Instagram asks). Add the location (your town) to posts 2, 5 and 9.
  2. Share each post to your Story. On posts 2, 5 and 9, add a Link sticker with https://tinyurl.com/invite-ig.
  3. Follow 10 of the venues on our lists, and like one or two of their recent posts. Don't message yet.
  4. Reply to any comment or DM the same day.

AI label: Instagram asks for the "AI info" label on realistic-looking AI images or video. These are clearly illustrated designs; it's your call whether to add it.
