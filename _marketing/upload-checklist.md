# Upload checklist

All files are in this folder. Each list below says which file goes where and which settings to pick. Do them in order; none of it needs any technical setup.

Status (Nov 6, 2026): Etsy listing, the 5 image pins, the video pin and the TikTok are all live. Instagram @inviteasart is on hold (new-account login hold). Wording rule: never "painted" — say custom / made to order / designed to your theme / inspired by the art you love.

## 1. Etsy listing

1. Open Etsy > Shop Manager > Listings > **Add a listing**.
2. **Photos**: upload the nine PNGs in this order: `etsy-01-hero.png`, `etsy-02-bachelorette.png`, `etsy-03-bridal-shower.png`, `etsy-04-welcome-drinks.png`, `etsy-05-rehearsal-dinner.png`, `etsy-06-rsvp.png`, `etsy-07-thanks.png`, `etsy-08-what-you-get.png`, `etsy-09-not-a-template.png`. The first one becomes the thumbnail.
3. **Video**: upload `etsy-video-1-sample.mp4` (the second clip, `etsy-video-2-cards.mp4`, is a spare; Etsy allows one video per listing, so keep it for Pinterest or Instagram).
4. **Title**: "Custom party invitation with RSVP, inspired by art you love, live link in 24 hours, no app for guests".
5. **About this listing**: choose **I designed it** ("Designed by" = you), **A finished product**, and **Made to order**.
6. **Type**: choose **Digital** (not physical). When Etsy asks how you deliver the file, choose **Made to order** so nothing is attached up front; you send the link by message after the buyer fills in the personalization box.
7. **Description**: paste the ready-to-paste text in the box below.
8. **Personalization**: switch it **ON**, make it **required**, and paste this prompt:
   "Tell me about your party: who it's for, date and time (or 2–3 dates to poll), place, what guests should know, one extra question for guests, and the style you want — your own theme, colors and mood, or an artist, a fashion house or an art style you love"
9. **Price**: `$15`. Quantity: 999 (digital, made to order).
10. **Processing time**: **1 day**.
11. **AI disclosure** (Etsy asks whether AI tools were used; also keep this line in the description): "Designed with AI tools under my creative direction; each invitation is designed to your description."
12. **Tags** (13 allowed, pick from): digital invitation, website invitation, invitation with rsvp, bridal shower invitation, bachelorette invitation, rehearsal dinner invitation, garden party invitation, animated invitation, art inspired invite, whatsapp invitation, evite alternative, custom invitation, party invite link.
13. Preview the listing on your phone, then **Publish**.

## 2. Pinterest

1. Make sure your Pinterest account is a **business account** (Settings > Account management > Convert to business). It is free and lets you add links and see stats.
2. Create a board called **Live party invitations with RSVP**.
3. Click **Create > Create Pin** and upload `pin-01-bachelorette.png`.
4. Copy the **Title** and **Description** for that pin from `pins.txt`.
5. In the **Link** box paste https://tinyurl.com/invite-pin and turn on **AI-modified**.
6. Choose the board **Live party invitations with RSVP** and click **Publish**.
7. Repeat steps 3 to 6 for `pin-02-bridal-shower.png`, `pin-03-welcome-drinks.png`, `pin-04-rehearsal-dinner.png` and `pin-05-sample.png`. Spacing them a day apart is fine.
8. Video pin: upload `etsy-video-2-cards.mp4`, use `pin-video-cover.png` as the cover, and the title/description under VIDEO PIN in `pins.txt`.
